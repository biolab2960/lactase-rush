What's what

Only pairs inside the glowing circle can reach your hands, like substrate that has diffused to the active site. Each split takes a moment, and your hands can't grab anything else while they work. New lactose is added as you split it, so [S] stays constant for the whole measurement.

Noncompetitive rounds: green rubber bands are the inhibitor. When one binds your wrists (the allosteric site), the active site changes shape and closes until the band pops off.
